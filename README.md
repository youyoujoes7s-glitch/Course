# Course Guide — MCP · Agentic AI · B2B

> Ranked by priority within each section. Priority goes to courses with **real hands-on labs** (not just lectures), whether free or paid.

**Legend:**
🆓 Free · 💰 Paid · 🏅 Official / Certified · 🧪 Includes a hands-on lab or project

---

## 🧩 MCP — Model Context Protocol

1. **[Anthropic Academy — Introduction to Model Context Protocol](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/introduction-to-model-context-protocol)** 🆓 🏅 🧪
   The official course from Anthropic itself. Build an MCP server and client from scratch in Python, and learn the three primitives (tools / resources / prompts) hands-on.

2. **[Anthropic Academy — MCP: Advanced Topics](https://anthropic.skilljar.com/model-context-protocol-advanced-topics)** 🆓 🏅 🧪
   The advanced part of the same official series: sampling, notifications, transports (stdio / StreamableHTTP), and production patterns.

3. **[DeepLearning.AI — MCP: Build Rich-Context AI Apps with Anthropic](https://learn.deeplearning.ai/courses/mcp-build-rich-context-ai-apps-with-anthropic)** 🆓 🏅 🧪
   Built in direct partnership with Anthropic, taught by Elie Schoppik (Head of Technical Education at Anthropic). Includes hands-on notebooks.

4. **[Udemy — MCP Crash Course: Complete Model Context Protocol in a Day (Eden Marco)](https://www.udemy.com/course/model-context-protocol/)** 💰 🧪
   Bestseller on Udemy, 8.5 hours and 90 lectures. Full hands-on build: servers, clients, and MCP security.

5. **[KodeKloud — Model Context Protocol Explained + Hands-on Lab](https://www.classcentral.com/classroom/youtube-what-is-mcp-model-context-protocol-explained-free-lab-495407)** 🆓 🧪
   Explanation plus an interactive lab that runs directly in the browser, no local setup needed.

6. **[freeCodeCamp — Intro to MCP Servers with Python (FastMCP)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DosHnyq78xY)** 🆓 🧪
   Three hands-on projects: a calculator over STDIO and HTTP Stream, API integration, and testing MCP with GitHub Copilot.

7. **[Hugging Face — MCP Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/mcp-course/unit0/introduction)** 🆓 🏅
   Official, free, certified course, with theory and practical exercises.

8. **[MCP — Model Context Protocol End-To-End Course 2026 (Playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL6tW9BrhiPTCDteflzehKS6Cn3a79-iCs)** 🆓
   A comprehensive playlist from fundamentals through deployment.

## 🤖 Agentic AI

1. **[Udemy — The Complete Agentic AI Engineering Course (Ed Donner)](https://www.udemy.com/course/the-complete-agentic-ai-engineering-course/)** 💰 🧪
   The strongest and most comprehensive course on the market right now. A 6-week program with 8 real projects (Career Digital Twin, Deep Research App, Engineering Team, Trading Platform...) covering the OpenAI Agents SDK, CrewAI, LangGraph, and AutoGen, ending with a full week on MCP.

2. **[Krish Naik — Complete Agentic AI Course In 10 Hours](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rV3HJ4LEZ7k)** 🆓 🧪
   Langchain, Langgraph, RAG, Vectorless RAG, Deep Agents, Guardrails, Evals, LLM Gateways — with GitHub repos for every section so you can build along.

3. **[freeCodeCamp — LangGraph Complete Course for Beginners](https://youtu.be/jGg_1h0qzaM)** 🆓 🧪
   3 hours, building 5 different agents from scratch in code, plus RAG integration.

4. **[Hugging Face — AI Agents Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/agents-course/unit0/introduction)** 🆓 🏅 🧪
   Official course with two certificates (Fundamentals and Full Completion), with graded assignments you need to complete.

5. **[Intellipaat — Complete Agentic AI Course Free 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3UKvRgfy2qY)** 🆓

6. **[Intellipaat — Agentic AI Full Course for Free 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wDs2egmmjFU)** 🆓

7. **[AI Agents Full Course 2026: Master Agentic AI (2 Hours)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EsTrWCV0Ph4)** 🆓
   A fast, practical summary if time is tight.

## 💼 B2B — Sales & Business Development

1. **[LinkedIn Learning — B2B Sales Foundations](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/b2b-sales-foundations)** 💰
   By Robbie Kellman Baxter. Covers the difference between B2B and B2C, and how to navigate multiple decision-makers inside one company.

2. **[Udemy — B2B Sales Masterclass: The Complete Course for Beginners](https://www.classcentral.com/course/udemy-b2b-sales-masterclass-the-complete-course-f-36970)** 💰 🧪
   A full playbook from strategy to closing, laid out as practical steps you can apply directly.

3. **[HubSpot Academy — Inbound Sales Certification](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/sales)** 🆓 🏅 🧪
   Globally recognized certification, entirely free, with practical exercises and ready-to-use templates.

4. **[FREE B2B Lead Generation Masterclass (Full Course)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWh3AT0ZCX8)** 🆓
   B2B lead generation from scratch — every channel, tool, and tactic that actually works.

5. **[The Ultimate Sales Training for 2026 [Full Course]](https://youtube.com/watch?v=StVqS0jD7Ls)** 🆓
   A complete sales course from first contact to closing.

6. **[Alison — Understanding B2B Development & Sales](https://alison.com/course/understand-b2b-business-development-and-sales)** 🆓
   A full introduction to B2B business development, from market identification to post-sale follow-up.
